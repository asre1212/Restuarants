Restaurants, ratings, and notes stay in this browser. Clearing site data or changing phones can erase them. Export a backup regularly; import it on your new phone.

Install, offline use & privacy

On iPhone, open this page in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Open the installed app once while online before relying on offline access. Use the installed app consistently: your browser and installed app may have separate storage.

The app downloads its own static files from its host. Your saved notebook is never uploaded. Optional assistant deliveries are held in a private delivery inbox and removed after this device saves them. Deliveries expire after seven days; expired packets are deleted on the next inbox check. The first device to import a delivery receives it; this is not cross-device sync. Website links open externally only when you tap them. Map tiles load from OpenStreetMap while online. Location lookups send only the restaurant name, city, and address to Photon by komoot; never your notes or ratings. Coordinates and lookup results stay on this device. Hosting providers may keep ordinary page-request logs.

Supported formats & assistant instructions

CSV header: name,city,cuisine,rating,notes,address,url,tags,michelinStars. Quote values containing commas or line breaks. Separate tags with semicolons. Without a header, each line is treated as a complete restaurant name. Ratings are never guessed.

Ask your assistant for a JSON packet with format "restaurant-log-import", version 1, and a restaurants array. Each restaurant has name, city, cuisine, rating (0–5 or null), notes, address, url, tags (array of strings), and michelinStars (0–3). Paste it here or import a file. Your assistant cannot read this notebook remotely. Use the connected Restaurants plugin to send new entries to the inbox, or paste them here.
