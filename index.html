Install, offline use & privacy

On iPhone, open this page in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Open the installed app once while online before relying on offline access. Use the installed app consistently: your browser and installed app may have separate storage.

The app downloads its own static files from its host. It never sends your restaurant data to a server. Website links open externally only when you tap them. Hosting providers may keep ordinary page-request logs.

Supported formats & assistant instructions

CSV header: name,city,cuisine,rating,notes,address,url,tags. Quote values containing commas or line breaks. Separate tags with semicolons. Without a header, each line is treated as a complete restaurant name. Ratings are never guessed.
